Privacy policy.

How VoltLedger handles data entered into its calculators and information generated by hosting or advertising.

What our calculators do

The calculator scripts operate in your browser. They do not require you to create an account, upload a utility bill, or provide a payment method. The Cost Simulator and Appliance Compare fields are processed locally on the page. The Standby Load Audit may save your entered device list in browser local storage so it remains available after a refresh.

You can remove that list using Clear All or by deleting this site's browser storage. CSV exports are generated locally when you choose Export CSV. We do not intentionally send those entries to our email account or collect them on a separate application server.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is deployed using a hosting provider. The provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses, browser metadata and error logs needed to deliver and secure the site. Its practices are governed by its own notices; we do not promise zero technical logging.

Google AdSense and cookies

This website includes a Google AdSense publisher script on content pages to support verification and potentially display advertising once approved. Google and its partners may process information, use cookies or similar technologies, and show ads according to applicable settings and consent requirements. Information about Google’s use of information from sites and apps is available at Google’s partner sites notice.

If legally required for a visitor's region, a suitable consent mechanism must be configured before personalized advertising is enabled. We do not claim a consent tool is already active when it is not.

Communications and contact

If you send an email to raflipanel2@gmail.com, the message and your address will be processed by email providers so we can respond. Do not send financial account credentials, full utility account numbers, or unnecessary personal details.

Changes

Published 11 November 2026. We may update this policy if our hosting, analytics or advertising setup materially changes.
