HOW IT WORKS

What the calculator models

The formula is watts ÷ 1,000 × hours per use day × days × units × active fraction. Cost is modeled kWh multiplied by your chosen rate. The rate field is not automatically updated. Fees, taxes, dynamic tariffs, and device changes are omitted.

Safety note

Never open electrical equipment, overload a plug monitor, or disconnect required medical/security devices based on a cost estimate. Ask a qualified professional when equipment or wiring is unsafe.
