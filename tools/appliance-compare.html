Enter the two options and select Compare.
HOW IT WORKS

Interpret payback with care

Annual energy savings are the difference in annual kWh multiplied by your chosen local rate. Simple payback divides the price premium by annual energy-only savings. The result ignores repair, taxes, future rate changes, finance and discounting.
