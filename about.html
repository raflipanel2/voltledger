About VoltLedger.

Who publishes VoltLedger and what its practical electricity guides are designed to do.

An independent educational field guide

VoltLedger is a small independent website about understanding home electricity usage. Its purpose is to turn confusing units, labels and bills into questions readers can investigate with transparent methods. It is not a utility company, retailer, auditor, licensed electrician or government agency.

We do not have access to your meter, electricity provider, accounts or home wiring. The tools estimate from values you enter. Our articles explain assumptions and encourage comparisons with real readings or official documents before spending money.

What makes a useful answer here?

Each editorial guide addresses a separate household decision: reading a bill, evaluating a purchase, interpreting a power label, or checking a suspected standby load. We include a worked example with explicit assumptions, practical steps, a misconception to avoid, and a link to source material.

The site does not rank or sell appliances and currently has no affiliate product links. Any advertising would be separate from editorial recommendations and subject to the site’s advertising disclosure.
