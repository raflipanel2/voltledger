Terms and safety notes.

Important scope and limitations for VoltLedger articles and calculators.

Educational use

VoltLedger provides general educational material and hypothetical energy calculations. It is not an electrical inspection, appliance certification, legal recommendation, financial forecast, or quotation from a utility. A formula can be arithmetically correct while its estimate is inaccurate if the entered usage or rate does not reflect reality.

Safety first

Do not open electrical panels or equipment, modify wiring, bypass protective devices, overload outlets, or use plug monitors beyond their ratings on the basis of this website. For faults, overheating, smoke, repeated breaker trips, or permanent electrical work, contact a qualified professional and follow applicable local rules and manufacturer instructions.

Accuracy and liability

We try to explain assumptions and link to reliable sources, but we cannot guarantee accuracy for every device, tariff, location or date. Verify critical details with the appropriate provider. Tools and articles may change as sources and software are updated.

Original content and reuse

Original text, diagrams and code are prepared for this publication. You may link to our pages and reference small excerpts with attribution, but may not reproduce entire articles or misrepresent the publisher.
