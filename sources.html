How to verify your local assumptions

Electricity rates should come from your current utility statement or published tariff. Appliance energy values should come from model-specific official labels or suitable measurement. Local electrical safety rules and manufacturer directions always take priority over general content here.

We deliberately avoid inventing current rates, regional rankings or test results. For the latest tariff or label requirements, consult the source that controls that information.
