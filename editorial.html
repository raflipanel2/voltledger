Editorial standards and corrections.

How VoltLedger develops estimates, cites primary sources and updates its content.

Our method

We aim to answer practical questions with the minimum number of assumptions required. Formula explanations follow established educational references such as Virginia Tech Extension, while consumer product comparisons use official FTC or ENERGY STAR documentation where relevant. We explain what a figure represents and what it leaves out.

Source priority

For technical concepts, we prefer original standards organizations, government programs, official product documentation and university extension resources. We do not use unattributed product rankings as evidence of energy savings. See the source directory for a short annotated list of these references.

Changes and corrections

Pages show a preparation date, not an assertion that a licensed professional verified a household. We update an article when a source changes, a calculation is wrong, or feedback reveals an unclear assumption. If a page is materially corrected, the page date should be updated during the edit.

Energy tariffs, model specifications and certification rules change by jurisdiction and over time. We do not publish automatically refreshed prices or pretend to observe live rates. Two Update Desk guides instead explain how readers can verify current terms with official providers.

Editorial and advertising separation

We do not rank products for payment or present a calculator result as a recommendation to purchase. Any ads are distinct from page content. Contact raflipanel2@gmail.com to report errors. No one associated with this website is presented as a licensed electrician or certified energy auditor.
